# Lessons

Binding rules for every trading session. A session may act against a rule only with a written justification in its journal entry naming the rule. Rules are added, amended, or retired ONLY by a review session, citing trade_ids. Candidate patterns without enough evidence live under "Watch patterns" until a review promotes or discards them.

Format: each entry is the rule, its evidence, and ONE `Status:` line. A review's re-check REPLACES the Status line; the old line moves to `journal/lessons_history.md` under the entry's id. Full text of every entry as of 2026-10-02, including all earlier re-check paragraphs: `journal/archive/lessons_full_2026-10-02.md`.

## Active

- **L1 - Size deliberately, and say why.** Position size is fully your decision (mandate v4 opened the whole paper account). Every entry states its sizing reasoning: why this notional given conviction, exit distance, and the rest of the book. Unexplained size is a process failure even when the trade wins. (Seed rule; amended 2026-08-27 when the size caps were removed.) Cite L1 itself, not the expired training directive, as the basis for sizing latitude.

- **L2 - The gate is right.** A structural denial means the idea was outside the contract; drop it. Never restructure an order to chase a denied idea. (Seed rule.)

- **L3 - No thesis, no trade.** If the thesis or its falsifiable exit condition cannot be written in two honest sentences, the trade does not exist. Holding is a position. (Seed rule.)

- **L5 - A ladder rung that fills is the thesis that actually happened, not the one written.** When a laddered entry fills only its highest, most extended rung while the lower confirmation rungs go unfilled, the base the thesis leaned on never confirmed: manage it as the weaker, chase-adjacent version (tighter stop toward the fill or nearest support, smaller add tolerance). Check which rung filled before trusting the original narrative. Evidence: t-2026-08-28-001 ESTC (only the 100.30 breakout rung filled; the 99.30/99.80 pullback rungs never did). Status: added 2026-08-28; single-trade evidence, revisit as ladder trades close.

- **L6 - Same-day time_stops need enough clock left to work.** A "flat by preclose today" time_stop on an entry after ~14:00 CT gives a multi-hour setup (base-and-reclaim, pullback-in-uptrend) under ~2.5 hours. For afternoon entries on setups that need time, extend the time_stop to the next session's open, or size and grade knowing the clock, not the thesis, may end it. Evidence: t-2026-08-28-001 ESTC, entered 14:20 CT and closed by its own time stop at 14:32 CT with neither stop nor target touched. Status: added 2026-08-28; single-trade evidence, revisit as afternoon entries close.

- **L7 - A trade is not bad until it is closed.** Unrealized drawdown inside the stop is the thesis still running. Never exit early because a position is red: the written stop, target, and time_stop ARE the decision. Equally, an open profit is not a win and never justifies extra risk elsewhere. Only CLOSED trades are evidence for reviews, stats, and watch patterns. When you close, close for a reason written in advance; "it was down" is not one. (Operator rule 2026-09-02.)

- **L8 - A probe's fill level needs an actual multi-touch defense, not a single reversal candle or a "buyers stepping in" narrative.** A level tested and held 2+ times (dojis/hammers/engulfings AT the same price) is real support; a level chosen because one reversal candle printed near it is a weaker bet even when everything else is done honestly - manage and size it as a C-probe. Evidence: t-2026-08-31-002 CRWD -$247.54 (opening-range low never tested before staging) and t-2026-09-01-008 STDN -$135.51 ("not a clean single-level defense"); contrast multi-touch probes ASND, CRCL, MRVL. Status: promoted from W1 on 2026-09-02 with 2 instances.

- **L9 - A name that has already outrun SPY by 10+ points over 20 days is still a candidate, but the thesis must cite L9 and say in writing why this one is different; size it knowing the bucket's record.** It is a disclosure and sizing rule, never an exclusion (operator rule 2026-09-24 - it had been read as a ban and kept META, +38% in a month, out of view). `trade_context.py --check` prints the bucket (SOP 4c); for `leader_10pp+`, give a concrete reason (a dated catalyst not yet in the 20-day move, a base reset); "momentum" or "sector rotation" alone is why the bucket lost. Prefer in-line-to-modest relative strength in ranking (0-10pp outperformers and names lagging SPY have carried the book). Caveats: a small, overlapping sample (same-symbol ladders: CRWD x3, TSLA x2), clustered 08-28 to 09-15, not proven causal, and in unresolved tension with s6a momentum, which selects recent leaders - s6a entries in this bucket carry the written exception. Retire if the bucket's p drifts back above 0.2, or if further wins push it above 0.05 (set by the 2026-09-30 review). Evidence: `by_rel_strength_20d.leader_10pp+` in `trend_signals.significant`; added 2026-09-23 at 0 of 16. Status (2026-10-08, 38 closed): 1 win in 18 (5.6% vs 55.0% for the rest), -$1,625.11, Fisher p 0.00141 vs Sidak 0.00183 (28 tests) - significant, slightly firmer. The new member is TXG t-2026-09-15-001 (+26.99pp, s6a, entered before L9 existed; -$35.70 after a +35.9% peak, so a leader that worked and was lost at the exit). Still binding; retire if further wins push p above 0.05.

## Watch patterns (candidate lessons, not yet binding)

Hypotheses a review named but could not yet support. They are NOT rules and are never cited as one. Every review re-checks each against the trades closed since, then promotes it, updates its Status line, or discards it with a reason. One with no new instance for 15 trading days is discarded as noise.

- **W4 - RSI14 at entry: 60-70 has lost almost every time; 45-60 has carried the book.** Measured on prior-close RSI (`trade_context --check`, `ta.py` `rsi14_prior_close`), never on `ta.py`'s live `rsi14`. Heavily entangled with L9 (leaders tend to sit at RSI 60-70). Promote if it crosses the Sidak line, or if it still separates winners from losers after excluding `leader_10pp+` names; discard if p drifts above 0.2 or after 15 trading days without support. Practical note until it resolves: a `strong_60-70` entry sizes below a `neutral_45-60` one. Status (2026-10-08, 38 closed): `strong_60-70` 1 of 12, -$962.84; `overbought_70+` 0 of 3, -$106.54 (TXG added at 70.4); RSI 60+ combined 1 of 15 vs the rest 11 of 23, Fisher p 0.012 (Sidak 0.00183, not crossed); outside the leader bucket still 0 of 3 (ASND, ARTY, PLTR); `neutral_45-60` 10 of 21, +$504.13, p 0.034 (suggestive). TXG is a leader-bucket name, so no new independent evidence; not promoted.

- **W7 - entries taken while SPY's own trend read "up" have done worse than entries in "mixed" markets.** Counter-intuitive (a tailwind should help) and entangled with L9 and W4 (the leader losses were bought in the late-August/early-September up tape). Promote only if it still separates after excluding `leader_10pp+` and RSI 60-70 entries; discard if p drifts above 0.2 or after 15 trading days (~2026-10-20) with no supporting close. Status (2026-10-08): `up` 3 of 18 (16.7%, -$858.15); `mixed` 9 of 20 (45.0%, +$278.84) after TXG (a `mixed` loser, a mild counter); `stock_up+market_up` 2 of 11 (18.2%, -$555.00); Fisher p 0.086; not in `trend_signals`. Discard ~2026-10-20 absent a supporting close.

- **W8 - s6a's two-lower-closes exit can give back a large open gain on a high-ATR name, and the replayed 1-ATR trail is the leading alternative.** Evidence: TXG t-2026-09-15-001 (mfe_atr 7.17, peak +35.9%, closed -3.15% after a -17% day on 10-06 and another lower close on 10-07; replayed trail +0.57%, half-off +0.93%). Across the book, `exit_replay.trail_1atr_after_1atr` is +$501.20 over 9 changed trades (6 losers saved, 2 winners hurt), paired_t 1.45. Counterweight: the playbook backtest (n=287, significant) found the first-down-close exit halves s6a's edge, so a tighter exit may cut winners more than it saves; the backtest's -3.98% average loss already includes giveback like TXG's. A trailing exit never overrides s6a without a lesson saying so (SOP 2d). Promote only at paired_t >= 2.5 with 10+ trades changed and the gain not carried by one trade; discard if paired_t falls below 0.5 or after 15 trading days (~2026-10-29) with no new s6a giveback close. Status (2026-10-08): added; 1 instance.

- **W9 - entries in names LAGGING SPY over 20 days have won most often.** The mirror of L9, not independent evidence. Promote if it crosses the Sidak line; discard if p drifts above 0.2 or after 15 trading days (~2026-10-29) with no new lagging-name close. Shapes ranking only, never excludes a name. Status (2026-10-08, 38 closed): `lagging_spy` 6 of 9 (66.7% vs 20.7% for the rest), +$419.21, Fisher p 0.0164 vs Sidak 0.00183 (suggestive); `outperforming_0-10pp` 5 of 11, +$626.59.
